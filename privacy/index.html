ApexAuto Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 policy effective date: September 6, 2026

Version 1.1 AI addition prepared: October 17, 2026; applies only to versions that offer optional AI assistance. This addition does not describe an AI feature in version 1.0.

ApexAuto is operated by Anhad Singh, an individual developer based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what data the ApexAuto iOS app collects, where it is stored, and who else can see it.

Who this app is for

ApexAuto is a business tool for automotive detailing, ceramic coating, and paint protection film shops. The person using the app (the shop owner or a shop employee, "you" or "the shop") enters information about the shop's own customers and vehicles. ApexAuto is not directed at, and is not intended to be used by, children under 13.

What the app stores

When you use ApexAuto, the app stores:

  • Shop profile — your shop's name, address, tax rate, service catalog, and bay configuration.
  • Your customers' information — names, phone numbers, email addresses, and, if you enter them, mailing addresses and notes.
  • Vehicle information — VINs, license plates, and odometer readings for vehicles you inspect.
  • Inspection evidence — photos taken during a vehicle walkaround, each with a burned-in timestamp and, if you allow location access, GPS coordinates; and the customer's signature captured on screen.
  • Invoices — line items, tax, tips, and a record of how a payment was made (for example, "e-Transfer" or "cash") and any reference number you enter. ApexAuto does not process card payments and never collects card numbers.

We do not ask your customers for any of this information directly — you, the shop, collect it from your own customers and enter it into the app. You are responsible for telling your customers what you record about them and for getting any consent your local law requires, including for photographing their vehicle and recording their signature.

Where this data is stored

The shop records above are stored on the device running the app. If you are signed into iCloud, records (shop profile, customer, vehicle, inspection, and invoice data) also sync to your private CloudKit database — the private iCloud storage tied to your own Apple ID. In versions 1.0 and 1.1, photos and PDF files stay on the device that captured them and are not synced to iCloud; only text records sync.

Version 1.0 has no developer-operated backend for your shop records. Its shop records are not sent to or stored by us. Version 1.1 adds the optional AI service described below; using it sends the specific context you review to that service and its processors. The normal intake, customer, schedule and invoice tools remain available without AI.

Third parties

The normal app tools use the following outside services for the purposes described. Optional AI in version 1.1 uses the additional processors named in the AI section below.

  • Apple — iCloud (private CloudKit database) for sync, and the App Store / StoreKit for managing your subscription. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • NHTSA vPIC (the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's Vehicle Identification Number decoding API) — when you look up a vehicle, ApexAuto sends only the 17-character VIN to https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov over HTTPS to retrieve the year, make, and model. No customer or shop information is sent with that request. If a VIN lookup is decoded offline (no network connection), nothing is sent anywhere.

ApexAuto contains no advertising SDKs or marketing analytics software and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers or advertisers. The optional AI service keeps limited security and usage records, described below, to operate the feature and enforce its limits.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the app until you delete it. Shop → Delete all data permanently removes shop, customer, vehicle, inspection, invoice, and photo data from the device. Because iCloud sync mirrors the on-device database, data deleted this way is also removed from your private CloudKit database. You can also delete ApexAuto's iCloud data at any time through Apple's iCloud storage settings, independent of the app.

Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data (including photos and PDFs) but does not, by itself, delete records already synced to your private CloudKit database — use Shop → Delete all data first, or manage iCloud storage directly through Apple.

Your choices

  • Location access is optional. If you decline it, evidence photos are still captured and timestamped, just without GPS coordinates.
  • Camera access is required for VIN scanning and photo capture, but every VIN field also accepts manual entry, and photo capture is not required to use the rest of the app.
  • iCloud sync follows the iCloud account signed into your device. Signing out of iCloud stops sync; it does not delete data already stored locally.

Optional AI assistance — version 1.1

AI is optional and needs an internet connection. Before your first AI request, the app asks permission to send reviewed context to Claude, provided by Anthropic, through the ApexAuto AI service hosted on Cloudflare. Before each request, the app shows the context that will be sent. You can edit or cancel it. Turn off Shop → AI settings → Allow reviewed AI requests to stop future requests. Turning it off cannot recall a request already sent.

What is sent and why

Depending on the task, the preview can contain your request and selected technician notes; vehicle year, make, model and size; and recorded condition descriptions. Quote and intake tasks can include the active service catalog with prices and durations. Message and day-plan tasks do not send that catalog. An invoice-reminder request can include the selected invoice's saved status, due date and service names; structured invoice totals, balances and payment references are excluded. Day planning uses anonymous job labels, booked times, statuses and service names, without vehicle details. Language and shop currency settings accompany the request. This context is used to draft service recommendations, an intake plan, a customer message or a day plan. Prices and totals displayed by ApexAuto are calculated by the app from your catalog and settings, rather than invented by AI.

The structured AI context excludes saved customer names and contact details, VINs, license plates, photos, signatures, GPS coordinates and payment reference numbers. The app also filters common identifiers from text. Filtering is imperfect: review pasted text and remove personal, confidential or unnecessary information before sending it. The shop remains responsible for any customer permission needed to submit customer information.

The service also receives a request identifier, an installation-specific Apple App Attest identifier and proof, and the technical network information needed to receive and protect the request. These are used to verify the app, prevent replay and abuse, enforce request limits and control service costs. There is no AI account registration and the provider API credential is not included in the app.

Processors and retention

  • Cloudflare hosts the ApexAuto AI service and processes requests in transit. Our application code does not save or log prompt or generated-draft content. Cloudflare may separately process network and security information, including IP addresses and traffic metadata, under its privacy policy. Disabling application logs does not mean that all Cloudflare infrastructure records are disabled.
  • Anthropic processes the reviewed context and generates the draft through its commercial API. Anthropic's standard API policy provides for deletion of inputs and outputs within 30 days, with exceptions including legal requirements, Usage Policy enforcement, different agreements and features with longer retention. Certain Covered Models have additional retention rules. ApexAuto does not promise zero retention. Anthropic says commercial inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default; voluntary feedback or training opt-ins can receive different treatment. ApexAuto does not submit in-app feedback to Anthropic.
  • Apple App Attest verifies that requests come from the expected app installation. Apple handles its part of this process under Apple's privacy policy.

ApexAuto's service stores verification identifiers and public keys, replay counters, timestamps, usage counts and request hashes/status needed for security and cost controls. It does not store the notes or returned drafts in that database. Scheduled cleanup removes request metadata within 33 days and installation records within 91 days of inactivity. Aggregated service spending totals are retained separately until at most three days after the corresponding UTC day or month ends; they cannot be used to recover your prompt or draft. Provider retention rules are separate. AI processing may take place outside your country, including the United States.

Removing access records and keeping drafts

Shop → AI settings → Remove AI service access records turns AI off and requests deletion of this installation's verification and usage records. A working connection and valid installation proof are required. If deletion fails, the app tells you; you can retry or contact us. Installation records that the app can no longer verify are subject to the inactivity cleanup above. Aggregate spending totals remain for the limited accounting window above so deletion cannot reset the service's cost limits. Removing these records does not delete information already processed under Anthropic's or Cloudflare's retention policies.

AI drafts remain in the current screen until you close or clear them. If you choose to save reviewed text to an intake, it becomes part of your shop records and follows the normal device/iCloud retention rules. If you share a draft, the destination you select handles that copy under its own policies. AI does not automatically send messages, book work, record customer approval or change an invoice. Check its output before using it.

ApexAuto Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

ApexAuto is operated by Anhad Singh, an individual developer based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what data the ApexAuto iOS app collects, where it is stored, and who else can see it.

Who this app is for

ApexAuto is a business tool for automotive detailing, ceramic coating, and paint protection film shops. The person using the app (the shop owner or a shop employee, "you" or "the shop") enters information about the shop's own customers and vehicles. ApexAuto is not directed at, and is not intended to be used by, children under 13.

What the app stores

When you use ApexAuto, the app stores:

  • Shop profile — your shop's name, address, tax rate, service catalog, and bay configuration.
  • Your customers' information — names, phone numbers, email addresses, and, if you enter them, mailing addresses and notes.
  • Vehicle information — VINs, license plates, and odometer readings for vehicles you inspect.
  • Inspection evidence — photos taken during a vehicle walkaround, each with a burned-in timestamp and, if you allow location access, GPS coordinates; and the customer's signature captured on screen.
  • Invoices — line items, tax, tips, and a record of how a payment was made (for example, "e-Transfer" or "cash") and any reference number you enter. ApexAuto does not process card payments and never collects card numbers.

We do not ask your customers for any of this information directly — you, the shop, collect it from your own customers and enter it into the app. You are responsible for telling your customers what you record about them and for getting any consent your local law requires, including for photographing their vehicle and recording their signature.

Where this data is stored

All of the data above is stored on the device running the app. If you are signed into iCloud, records (shop profile, customer, vehicle, inspection, and invoice data) also sync to your private CloudKit database — the private iCloud storage tied to your own Apple ID. In this version of ApexAuto, photos and PDF files stay on the device that captured them and are not synced to iCloud; only text records sync.

ApexAuto does not have its own servers. In this version, no data you enter is sent to or stored by us. We cannot see it.

Third parties

ApexAuto shares data with two outside services, and only for the purpose described:

  • Apple — iCloud (private CloudKit database) for sync, and the App Store / StoreKit for managing your subscription. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • NHTSA vPIC (the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's Vehicle Identification Number decoding API) — when you look up a vehicle, ApexAuto sends only the 17-character VIN to https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov over HTTPS to retrieve the year, make, and model. No customer or shop information is sent with that request. If a VIN lookup is decoded offline (no network connection), nothing is sent anywhere.

ApexAuto contains no analytics software, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the app until you delete it. Shop → Delete all data permanently removes shop, customer, vehicle, inspection, invoice, and photo data from the device. Because iCloud sync mirrors the on-device database, data deleted this way is also removed from your private CloudKit database. You can also delete ApexAuto's iCloud data at any time through Apple's iCloud storage settings, independent of the app.

Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data (including photos and PDFs) but does not, by itself, delete records already synced to your private CloudKit database — use Shop → Delete all data first, or manage iCloud storage directly through Apple.

Your choices

  • Location access is optional. If you decline it, evidence photos are still captured and timestamped, just without GPS coordinates.
  • Camera access is required for VIN scanning and photo capture, but every VIN field also accepts manual entry, and photo capture is not required to use the rest of the app.
  • iCloud sync follows the iCloud account signed into your device. Signing out of iCloud stops sync; it does not delete data already stored locally.
